Association Smart YIRIBA

Smart YIRIBA is a non-profit association founded and registered in Timbuktu, Mali. It works to expand digital access, support training and employment for young people and women, encourage entrepreneurship and strengthen communities.

Featured programme

Smart Women
Academy

Smart Women Academy is a Smart YIRIBA programme focused on young women’s skills in entrepreneurship, computing and leadership. It aims to support their personal development and entry into employment.
