Smart Women Academy

Smart Women Academy is a Smart YIRIBA programme focused on young women’s skills in entrepreneurship, computing and leadership. It aims to support their personal development and entry into employment.

Contact the programme team for information about upcoming sessions, their content and how to take part.

Topics presented for Smart Women Academy

  • Entrepreneurship training
  • Computing
  • Leadership
  • Personal development
  • Company visits
  • Incubation
  • Mentoring
  • Support with business creation
