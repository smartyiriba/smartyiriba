About

Our identity, our history and our presence in Mali.

Who are we?

Association SMART/YIRIBA is the legal name of Smart YIRIBA. Established in Timbuktu in 2018, the association carries out training and support activities in Bamako.

Institutional status

  • Apolitical association
  • Non-denominational
  • Non-profit
  • Unlimited duration
  • Operates throughout Mali

Articles 1, 3, 4 and 7 of the statutes

Our history

  • 2 May 2018 — Declaration date stated on the registration receipt.
  • 15 May 2018 — Statutes drawn up and signed in Timbuktu.
  • 28 December 2018 — Registration receipt issued.
